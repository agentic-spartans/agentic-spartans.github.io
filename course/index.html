Questions

Which computer do I need?

A Mac, Windows or Linux computer you may install programs on. OpenCode has no Chromebook or iPad version. Without one you can still watch the videos and read the example problems.

Where should my Studio folder go?

Make a folder named Studio in your home folder (the one with your name; on a Mac, open Finder and press Shift-Command-H) and unzip the course files into it (on a Mac, move the zip into Studio, then double-click it there). Not in Desktop, Documents, Downloads, OneDrive or iCloud: there your computer may ask OpenCode for extra permissions and a sync service can get in the way.

How do I install OpenCode on a Mac?

Download the OpenCode desktop app from opencode.ai/desktop: macOS (Apple Silicon) for a Mac with an M chip, macOS (Intel) for an older Mac (Apple menu › About This Mac shows the chip). Open the downloaded file, then drag OpenCode into Applications. If it will not open, click Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security. Then follow video 2 from adding your key (at 0:40).

I use Linux. How do I install OpenCode?

Download the OpenCode desktop .deb file (Ubuntu, Debian) or .rpm file (Fedora) from opencode.ai/desktop (x64 for most laptops) and double-click it to install it. Then follow video 2 from adding your key (at 0:40).

Where do I get a key, or help?

Student course: your key, with credit already on it, is on the Get your key page in Module 1 of the course's D2L page; you pay nothing. Shorter version and anyone else: make an account on openrouter.ai, buy credit, then make a key and give it a spending limit; in the shorter version a key is recommended for practice, not required. Without credit, the first run fails.

OpenCode says the port is in use?

Close its other windows and try again.
