Spartan Agentic AI Studio

Problems

These are real problems from faculty across MSU and from the Studio, the group that makes this course. Each problem comes with an AI agent team. The team is a folder you download and open in OpenCode. Each page shows the problem, how its team works, what a good answer needs, and what the team found when we tried it. In the student course, the costs per run come out of the credit on the course key.

Words with a dotted underline, like agent team, are explained in videos 1 to 3.
